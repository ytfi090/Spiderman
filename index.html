HISTORY
Interconnected Universe

The Saga Scope

The "Spider-Man game saga" refers to the modern, critically acclaimed Insomniac Games Universe (Earth-1048), which began in 2018. While there are dozens of older games dating back to 1982, the current "Saga" is defined by its interconnected narrative, consistent voice cast, and unified visual identity.

Over the course of three major releases, Insomniac has redefined superhero storytelling, moving away from simple origin stories to focus on the heavy emotional cost of wearing the mask.

File 01: The Beginning

Marvel's Spider-Man (2018)

The Narrative Arc

A 23-year-old Peter Parker, who has been Spider-Man for eight years, helps the police arrest Wilson Fisk (Kingpin). This creates a power vacuum filled by the "Inner Demons," led by Mister Negative (Martin Li). Peter’s mentor, Dr. Otto Octavius, loses his funding and descends into villainy, forming the Sinister Six. They release a bioweapon called Devil's Breath on NYC.

The climax is one of the most emotional in gaming history: Peter is forced to choose between using a cure to save his dying Aunt May or using it to save the thousands of infected citizens. He chooses the city, sacrificing his only family.

Visuals & Gameplay

Introduced the "Momentum-Based Swing" system. Visually, the game was a technical showcase for the PS4, utilizing advanced skin shaders and high-fidelity facial animations to bring the characters to life. The "Suit Power" system allowed players to customize their combat style significantly.

DLC: The City That Never Sleeps

PART 01

The Heist

Introduces Felicia Hardy (Black Cat). She leads Peter on a chase through the city, tricking him into believing she has a son to get his help stealing Maggia data drives.

PART 02

Turf Wars

Focuses on Hammerhead. He uses stolen Sable International tech to wage a brutal war against the Maggia families. This chapter sees Yuri Watanabe begin her descent into vigilantism.

PART 03

Silver Lining

Silver Sable returns to reclaim her tech. Peter teams up with her to defeat a "cyborg" Hammerhead. The saga concludes with Miles Morales beginning his training.

EVOLVE
File 02: The New Spark

Miles Morales (2020)

The Story of Harlem

Set one year after the first game, Peter goes to Symkaria, leaving 17-year-old Miles as the city's only Spider-Man. A war breaks out between the Roxxon Energy Corporation and a high-tech criminal army called The Underground, led by the Tinkerer (Miles' childhood friend, Phin Mason).

Miles discovers unique "Venom" (bio-electricity) and camouflage powers. The story ends with Phin sacrificing herself to prevent an energy explosion that would have destroyed Harlem.

TECHNICAL_UPGRADE

This was the PS5 launch title that showcased Ray-Traced Reflections and near-instant loading. The animation work was updated to show Miles' "inexperience"—his swings are flailing and less composed than Peter's, reflecting his growth.

TOGETHER
File 03: Dual Heroes

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (2023)

The Great Hunt & The Symbiote

Peter and Miles team up to face Kraven the Hunter. Meanwhile, Harry Osborn returns, seemingly cured by a mysterious black suit (the Symbiote). The suit eventually bonds with Peter, making him aggressive and powerful.

The game explores the corruption of power. After Peter removes the suit, it bonds with Harry, transforming him into Venom. After a city-wide invasion, the Spiders defeat Venom, leaving Harry in a coma and Peter taking a hiatus from the mask.

GAMEPLAY_REVOLUTION

Introduced "Web Wings" for gliding across the river and "Switching" between Peter and Miles instantly. The visuals utilized full SSD speed for the fastest traversal ever seen in an open-world game.
