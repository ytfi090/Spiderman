2018 Peter Parker Open World GOTY 2018
Narrative

The Story

Eight years into his career, Spider-Man is a seasoned hero — but life as Peter Parker is still a mess. What follows is one of gaming's most emotional superhero stories.

01
THE BEGINNING — Rise of Mr. Negative
Peter Parker, 23, works at a science lab under Dr. Otto Octavius while moonlighting as Spider-Man. He helps capture Wilson Fisk, only for a power vacuum to emerge. Martin Li — philanthropist and head of the F.E.A.S.T. shelters — is secretly Mr. Negative, a crime lord seeking revenge on Norman Osborn for destroying his village and killing his parents. Li activates Devil's Breath — a modified pathogen — planning to release it across New York.
02
THE MIDDLE — Sinister Six
Otto Octavius, rejected by Oscorp after years of unpaid work, recruits the Sinister Six: Vulture (Adrian Toomes), Electro (Max Dillon), Scorpion (Mac Gargan), Rhino (Aleksei Sytsevich), and Mr. Negative. Their goal: steal Devil's Breath and release it citywide to punish Osborn. Peter discovers his mentor Otto is the Octopus, a devastating betrayal. The Six break out of Ryker's Island, wreaking havoc across New York.
⚡ Plot Twist
Dr. Otto Octavius — Peter's trusted mentor and scientific idol — is revealed as Doctor Octopus, the mastermind behind the Sinister Six. His neural interface, designed to cure his degenerative disease, has slowly warped his mind.
03
THE ENDING — The Greatest Sacrifice
Devil's Breath is released. Among the infected is Aunt May, who contracted it while working at F.E.A.S.T. The only cure — Norman Osborn's personal antidote — exists in one dose. Peter defeats Otto in a heartbreaking confrontation, but faces an impossible choice: use the antidote on May or manufacture enough for the city. May has always known Peter is Spider-Man. In her final moments, she tells him she's proud, and dies. Peter ensures the antidote reaches the city. In a devastating post-credits scene, Miles Morales — bitten by a genetically-enhanced Oscorp spider — begins his own journey.
💔 Emotional Arc
Aunt May's death is one of gaming's most powerful moments. Her final words — "I know. I've always known. And I am so proud of you." — recontextualize the entire game and cement Peter's sacrifice as the defining act of his character.

Antagonists

Villains & Sinister Six

Otto Octavius
Doctor Octopus · Primary Antagonist
Backstory
A brilliant scientist and Peter's mentor, Otto spent years developing revolutionary prosthetic neural interfaces at Oscorp, only to be dismissed and have his work stolen by Norman Osborn. Suffering from a degenerative neurological disease, the neural interface he created to save himself slowly corrupts his mind.

Otto forms and leads the Sinister Six, manipulating each member's grievance against Osborn. He releases Devil's Breath not for money, but for revenge — making him a tragic villain with genuine cause.

Li weaponizes Devil's Breath and his Inner Demons gang to attack Osborn, terrorizing New York in the process. His corruption of Aunt May's F.E.A.S.T. shelter is a personal blow to Peter.

Mechanics

World & Gameplay

🕸️
Web-Swinging
Physics-based momentum swinging through all five boroughs of Manhattan. Swing angles, altitude, and speed all factor into traversal. Tricks and diving enhance movement. Widely praised as the best traversal in superhero gaming.
🥊
Freeform Combat
Aerial, ground, and web-based combo system inspired by Batman: Arkham. Gadgets like Impact Webs, Web Bombs, and Trip Mines can be chained mid-combo. Focus meter powers both healing and finisher takedowns.
🗺️
Open World NYC
All of Manhattan rendered in stunning detail. Includes Central Park, Times Square, the Brooklyn Bridge, and dozens of real landmarks. 350+ collectibles, 45+ crimes, 23 side missions, and countless photo opportunities.
🥷
Stealth Sections
MJ and Miles have stealth-focused infiltration sections that diversify gameplay. Players use the environment to distract guards and solve puzzles while maintaining a civilian perspective on the Spider-Man world.
🔬
Research Stations
Peter's scientific background is woven into gameplay through research stations, science puzzles, and Oscorp tower challenges. Token-based upgrade system for suits, gadgets, and suit powers.
📡
Drone Challenges
Spider-Man sets up FEAST-linked drone towers to aid with crime monitoring. Time trial races, aerial challenges, and combat arenas provide skill-based content across the map.

MILES
MORALES

2020 Miles Morales Venom Powers Launch Title
Narrative

The Story

A boy from Harlem becomes the hero his community needs. Miles Morales tells a deeply personal story of identity, family, and what it means to be Spider-Man — on your own terms.

01
BEGINNING — A New Spider-Man
One year after the Devil's Breath crisis, Miles Morales has been trained by Peter and is ready to start operating on his own. Set during Christmas, Peter leaves for Symkaria with MJ, leaving Miles responsible for protecting New York. Miles' neighborhood of Harlem becomes caught between two warring powers: Roxxon Energy Corporation — a corrupt mega-corporation — and the Underground, a techno-anarchist movement led by the Tinkerer. When Roxxon's experimental energy source (Nuform) is stolen in a dramatic opening explosion, the war for Harlem begins.
02
MIDDLE — The Underground's Truth
Miles discovers the Tinkerer is actually Phin Mason — his childhood best friend, whom he believed died in a lab accident. Phin blames Roxxon for killing her brother Rick, whose research into Nuform Roxxon stole. The Underground's attack on Roxxon is a Robin Hood-style revenge mission. Meanwhile, Roxxon's CEO Simon Krieger is revealed as a ruthless corporatist willing to endanger all of Harlem to protect his Nuform investment. Miles' uncle Aaron Davis (the Prowler) is forced to hunt Miles by Krieger.
⚡ Plot Twist
The Tinkerer is revealed to be Phin Mason — Miles' best friend, believed dead. Her mission is righteous but her methods become increasingly dangerous, culminating in a plan that would destroy all of Harlem to prove Nuform's danger.
03
ENDING — The Ultimate Sacrifice
Nuform's reactor is destabilized and will obliterate Harlem. Phin, realizing the cost of her revenge, sacrifices herself to fly the reactor into the atmosphere, dying in the explosion. Miles uses his Venom Blast to overload and power down the reactor before it detonates. He collapses — seemingly dead — but is revived by the combined love of his community. Miles unmasks publicly in Harlem and is cheered as their Spider-Man. His mother Rio Morales — a city council candidate — sees her son's heroism and embraces it.
💔 Emotional Core
The game's emotional peak: Miles and Phin reconcile in the reactor chamber. Phin's sacrifice mirrors the parental loss they both share. Miles' near-death and community embrace echoes the death of his father Jefferson in the first game — transforming grief into purpose.

Mechanics

Unique Gameplay

⚡
Venom Powers
Miles' bio-electric Venom abilities are entirely unique to him. Venom Punch stuns and disrupts enemies, Venom Dash teleports through combat, Venom Jump sends shockwaves across large areas, and the Mega Venom Blast nukes all nearby enemies at massive focus cost.
👻
Camouflage
Miles can become completely invisible for a period, allowing completely different stealth approaches impossible for Peter. Enemies exposed to camouflage-broken attacks are stunned. Combat invisibility enables unique ambush strategies.
🏙️
Harlem Setting
The game focuses on Harlem and upper Manhattan — a smaller, more intimate map than SM2018. Every block feels personal to Miles. Roxxon checkpoints and Underground hideouts transform the neighborhood over the story's course.
🎵
Hip-Hop Soundtrack
The game's acclaimed soundtrack blends orchestral superhero themes with hip-hop, R&B, and Afro-Latin influences reflecting Miles' identity. Artists like Lecrae, Lute, and Swae Lee contribute, making traversal feel culturally grounded.
📱
Activity System
Miles' heroism is rooted in community — activities include helping neighbors, catching criminals for locals, and completing app-based missions. The activity system rewards player engagement with unique suit mods and emotional narrative payoffs.
❄️
Holiday New York
Set at Christmas, the game features a snow-covered Manhattan with decorations, carolers, and seasonal atmosphere. One of the most beloved aesthetic choices in PlayStation gaming — swinging through snow-swept New York at night is magical.

MAN 2

2023 Peter & Miles Symbiote GOTY Nominee
Narrative

The Story

The most ambitious entry in the series. Two Spider-Men, one symbiote, and a hunter who turns New York into a killing field. A story about identity, corruption, and whether good men can survive what they become.

01
BEGINNING — Kraven Arrives
Peter Parker is fighting a degenerative neurological disease linked to his spider-bite. Miles is now a fully operational Spider-Man at university. Kraven the Hunter — an obsessed apex predator who has hunted every creature on Earth — arrives in New York seeking the ultimate challenge: supervillains. He systematically hunts and either kills or captures every known Marvel villain in New York, including Vulture, Sandman, Scorpion, and the Lizard. Kraven sees death in battle as the only worthy end, and is determined to find someone who can kill him.
02
MIDDLE — The Black Suit
Dr. Curt Connors (the Lizard) was hiding an alien symbiote specimen obtained from the crashed meteor that created Connors' condition. Kraven's hunt releases it. The symbiote bonds with Peter, dramatically amplifying his power while curing his neurological disease — but feeding his aggression, arrogance, and worst impulses. Peter in the black suit becomes increasingly violent and emotionally unavailable to MJ, Harry, and Miles. He defeats Kraven's soldiers with terrifying efficiency but begins to lose himself. Meanwhile, Harry Osborn — Norman's son, kept alive for years by his own symbiote "cure" — begins to bond fully with his symbiote Venom.
🖤 Plot Twist
Harry Osborn's life-support system has been a symbiote all along. Norman Osborn, desperate to save his dying son, made a deal with a creature more dangerous than anything New York has faced. Harry is not just hosting Venom — he IS Venom, and his love for his father has been slowly weaponized.
03
ENDING — The Dual Spider-Men
Miles separates Peter from the symbiote in a brutal confrontation. Peter, cured but humbled, acknowledges what the suit made him. Harry — fully consumed as Venom — begins transforming all of New York into a symbiote ecosystem for a creature called the Hive Mind. Peter and Miles team up in the ultimate final battle. Peter faces Kraven, now voluntarily bonded with a symbiote and the most powerful he's ever been. Miles faces Venom-Harry while protecting New York. The ending is bittersweet: Harry is separated from Venom but left in a coma, Norman is shattered, and Peter finally proposes to MJ. The post-credits tease a returning villain and the birth of an Anti-Venom symbiote. Peter steps back to let Miles lead.
💔 Emotional Arc
The game's true emotional core: Harry Osborn's tragedy. A young man who has been fighting a terminal illness all his life, kept alive by something monstrous his father put inside him. His friendship with Peter — the only person who always believed in him — makes his corruption as Venom one of the series' most devastating moments.

Role in Game
Kraven systematically dismantles New York's villain ecosystem, hunting the Lizard, Vulture, Sandman, Scorpion, and more. His Kravinoff Foundation serves as cover while his hunters occupy the city. He is simultaneously antagonist and tragic figure.

Two boss encounters. The mid-game brawl showcases Kraven's peak-human abilities. The finale features symbiote-enhanced Kraven in a gladiatorial arena of his own design — the culmination of his death wish. Peter grants him neither the glory nor the mercy he seeks.

Harry evolves from a heartwarming return to a terrifying Venom across the game's second half. His corrupted mission — infecting New York as an act of love — makes him one of the most morally complex antagonists in superhero gaming.
Boss Fight
The final confrontation with Venom is a multi-phase spectacular — ground combat, aerial symbiote combat, and a final moment where Miles uses his Venom Blast to separate Harry from the symbiote. Peter must then contain it alone in a devastating sacrifice callback.

Mechanics

New Features

🦅
Web Wings
Both Spider-Men can glide across Manhattan using retractable web wings — essentially surfing the city's wind currents. Dramatically expands traversal and allows covering miles of city in seconds when combined with Web Swinging.
🛡️
Parry System
A new timed parry mechanic adds depth to combat. Perfect parries stun enemies, deflect projectiles, and create openings for devastating counter-attacks. Kraven's boss design is specifically built around parry mastery.
🗽
Expanded NYC
The map expands to include Brooklyn and Queens for the first time, nearly doubling the playable area. Each borough has unique visual identity: Queens' residential blocks, Brooklyn's bridges and brownstones, and a contaminated symbiote district in Midtown.
🔄
Dual Hero Switching
The game allows switching between Peter and Miles at any point in the open world — each with unique move sets, gadgets, and traversal styles. Story missions alternate protagonists, forcing players to master both playstyles.
🖤
Symbiote Powers
Peter's black suit grants a completely different combat identity — brutal, aggressive, and overwhelming. Symbiote tendrils grab multiple enemies simultaneously, pull them from cover, and execute devastating slam combos unavailable in normal suits.
📸
