Peter Parker

Miles Morales

The New Spider-Man

Peter’s apprentice, now a full-fledged hero with unique bio-electric Venom powers and camouflage abilities.

The Insomniac Difference

  • 🕸️

    Unmatched Traversal

    The standard-bearer for superhero movement. Swinging through Insomniac's NYC feels fluid, intuitive, and endlessly satisfying, augmented by web wings in the sequel.

  • ❤️

    Heartfelt Narrative

    They understand that the best Spider-Man stories are about the man beneath the mask. The narrative weight of Peter and Miles' personal lives is as compelling as the boss fights.

  • 💥

    Dynamic Combat

    A perfect blend of acrobatic improvisation, gadget gameplay, and unique powers (like Miles' bio-electricity) that makes you feel exactly like Spider-Man.
