File 01: 2018

Marvel's Spider-Man

The Story: Peter Parker has been the wall-crawler for 8 years. He's experienced but exhausted. The story is a masterclass in duality—showing Peter's failing personal life against his success as a hero. The fall of Otto Octavius is tragic; he isn't just a villain, but a father figure who breaks Peter's heart.

Visuals4K HDR recreation of NYC, featuring a living, breathing Manhattan with iconic landmarks.
GameplayPhysics-based swinging that uses momentum and gravity to feel authentic.

The DLC: City That Never Sleeps

This expansion feels like a gritty crime novel. It introduces Black Cat, adding a layer of romantic tension and deception. It also explores the militarization of NYC through Silver Sable and the terrifying transformation of Hammerhead into a cyborg mob boss.

Character Dossiers (Game 1 & DLC)

Yuri Lowenthal

Peter Parker

The veteran Spidey. He is agile, witty, but emotionally strained. His journey in the first game is about learning that he can't save everyone, culminating in the loss of Aunt May.

William Salyers

Otto Octavius

A brilliant scientist whose neurological decay and resentment toward Norman Osborn drive him to lead the Sinister Six. He is the saga's most personal antagonist.

Erica Lindbeck

Black Cat

A master thief and Peter's former flame. She plays a dangerous game of "catch me if you can," using Peter’s sense of responsibility against him in the DLC.

Stephen Oyoung

Mister Negative

Martin Li is a man split in two. A philanthropist by day and a vengeful criminal by night. His "Negative Energy" represents the corruption of grief.

File 02: 2020

Spider-Man: Miles Morales

The Story: A more intimate, focused tale. Set during Christmas in Harlem, Miles must find his own identity as a hero. It’s a story about community and the weight of secrets, as his best friend Phin becomes his greatest enemy.

VisualsRay-tracing on PS5 brings the snowy streets and neon lights of Harlem to life with incredible reflections.
GameplayIntroduces "Venom Power" (bio-electricity) and Camouflage, adding stealth and explosive combat options.
Nadji Jeter

Miles Morales

Young, earnest, and musically inclined. Miles struggles with the "Spider-Man" mantle, eventually realizing he doesn't need to be Peter—he needs to be himself.

Jasmine Savoy Brown

Phin Mason

The Tinkerer. Driven by revenge against Roxxon for the death of her brother, she leads a high-tech army. Her tragedy is her inability to see past her anger.

Ike Amadi

The Prowler

Aaron Davis, Miles' uncle. He walks a gray line between protecting his nephew and working for the highest bidder. He is Miles' cautionary tale.

File 03: 2023

Marvel's Spider-Man 2

The Story: The scale explodes. Two playable Spiders, an expanded map including Queens and Brooklyn, and the arrival of Kraven. The narrative explores addiction and power through the Symbiote suit, which corrupts Peter before birthing the monstrous Venom.

Tony Todd

Venom

A hulking, terrifying presence. Not just a monster, but a dark reflection of Peter and Harry's desire to "heal the world" through force.

Jim Pirri

Kraven the Hunter

A dying man looking for a warrior’s death. He treats NYC as his personal hunting ground, killing several villains from the first game to prove his dominance.

Graham Phillips

Harry Osborn

Peter’s best friend. His return is the emotional anchor of the game. His desperation to live leads him to bond with the Symbiote, with catastrophic results.

Mark Whitten

Dr. Curt Connors

The Lizard. A tragic scientist who Peter and Miles must save while he is in a feral, monstrous state. He provides key insights into the Symbiote's origin.
