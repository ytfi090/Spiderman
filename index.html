Marvel's Spider-Man 2

The pinnacle. Dual protagonists—Peter Parker and Miles Morales—face Kraven the Hunter and the arrival of the Symbiote. The 1048 Census

A 12-subject personnel dossier including protagonist heroes, antagonists, allies, and civilians whose arcs define the saga. Each entry includes voice actor, role classification, character summary, and narrative importance.

Voice: Yuri Lowenthal

Peter Parker / Spider-Man

The veteran hero. 23-year-old lab assistant turned crime-fighter. Carries the weight of Aunt May's death and his responsibility to the city. Battles guilt, sacrifice, and the corrupting influence of the Symbiote suit. His arc spans from hero to antihero, culminating in his transformation into Venom before redemption.

ROLE: Primary Protagonist | STATUS: Active | ALIASES: Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man
Voice: Nadji Jeter

Miles Morales / Spider-Man

The evolved hero. 16-year-old from Brooklyn with bio-electric powers. Trained by Peter Parker, he must protect Harlem from Roxxon Energy while navigating his identity as a bi-racial teenager in a city on fire. His journey is one of self-discovery, determination, and finding his own legacy.

ROLE: Secondary Protagonist | STATUS: Ascending | ALIASES: Venom Strike, Electric Conduit
Voice: Tony Todd

Venom / Harry Osborn

A living, breathing nightmare born from the marriage of the Symbiote and Harry Osborn's dying body. The entity seeks to "heal the world" through violent conquest. Powerful, chaotic, intelligent—and utterly consumed by alien ambition and human rage. Spider-Man 2's primary threat.

ROLE: Antagonist | THREAT LEVEL: Extinction | STATUS: Contained
Voice: Jim Pirri

Kraven the Hunter

Sergei Kravinoff. A man dying of cancer who views New York City as his final hunt. Leads an army of enhanced hunters in pursuit of Spider-Man, treating the city as a safari. His obsession stems from respect and a desire for one final, worthy opponent before death claims him.

ROLE: Primary Antagonist (SM2) | THREAT LEVEL: Extreme | STATUS: Defeated
Voice: Laura Bailey

MJ Watson

Daily Bugle reporter and Peter's confidante. A strategist who investigates crime syndicates while helping Peter dismantle enemy bases. Her investigative skills and emotional intelligence make her essential to the narrative, though her lack of powers forces her into ethical dilemmas.

ROLE: Ally / Investigator | STATUS: Active | AFFILIATION: Daily Bugle
Voice: Erica Lindbeck

Felicia Hardy / Black Cat

Professional thief with a complicated history with Peter. Her white hair and luck-based powers make her a formidable force. She lies, manipulates, and steals—but beneath the deception lies genuine affection for Peter. Her DLC chapter explores the fine line between hero and criminal.

ROLE: Morally Gray | STATUS: Ambiguous | AFFILIATION: Independent
Voice: Tara Platt

Yuri Watanabe / Wraith

Former Police Captain. Trauma from losing her squad to Hammerhead's cyborg forces drives her transformation into the vigilante Wraith. She operates outside the law using extreme methods, creating moral friction with Spider-Man. Her descent into violence is one of the saga's most compelling character arcs.

ROLE: Anti-Hero | STATUS: Uncontrollable | AFFILIATION: NYPD (estranged)
Voice: Graham Phillips

Harry Osborn

Peter's best friend since high school. Secretly kept in a stasis tank by Norman Osborn due to a genetic disease. When released, Harry is desperate to survive, leading him to seek a cure at any cost. His bond with Peter drives central plot threads, and his infection by the Symbiote sets up Spider-Man 2's climax.

ROLE: Personal Catalyst | STATUS: Transformed | AFFILIATION: Osborn Industries
Voice: Stephen Oyoung

Martin Li / Mister Negative

A man of duality. Once driven by revenge against the Oscorp corporation, Martin Li was manipulated into becoming the supervillain Mister Negative. His redemption arc in Spider-Man 2 shows a complex antagonist seeking penance, using his powers for good—though Spider-Man initially refuses to trust him.

ROLE: Reformed Villain | STATUS: Redemption Path | THREAT LEVEL: Low
Voice: Griffin Puatu

Ganke Lee

Miles' best friend and tech genius. Designed the Spider-Man App that helps Miles navigate his double life. His humor balances darker moments, and his loyalty to Miles is unwavering. Represents the tech-savvy millennial generation navigating superhero reality.

ROLE: Comic Relief / Tech Support | STATUS: Confidant | AFFILIATION: Miles' Inner Circle
Voice: Jacqueline Pinol

Rio Morales

Miles' mother and City Council member. The emotional anchor of Miles' story. She works tirelessly to improve Harlem, often at odds with corporate interests. Her protective love for Miles and her community activism define her character, making her one of the saga's most grounded presences.

ROLE: Maternal Figure | STATUS: Active | AFFILIATION: Harlem Community
Voice: William Salyers

Otto Octavius / Doctor Octopus

The brilliant scientist and mentor. His tragic accident fused his artificial limbs with his nervous system, creating the monster Doc Ock. Imprisoned in the Raft, planning revenge against Spider-Man. Otto's fall from grace—from hero-worship to nihilism—mirrors Peter's potential dark path.

ROLE: Tragic Antagonist | STATUS: Imprisoned | THREAT LEVEL: High (Contained)
