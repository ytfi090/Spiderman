Entry 01 | 2018

Marvel's Spider-Man

The foundation of Earth-1048. Peter Parker is 23 years old, working as a lab assistant for Otto Octavius. The story begins with the arrest of Wilson Fisk, which triggers a gang war between the Maggia and the Inner Demons. The narrative focuses on the fall of Doc Ock and the release of Devil's Breath.

Visual Engine

Custom engine features dynamic crowd AI and photorealistic HDR recreation of Manhattan.

Core Loop

Introduced physics-based momentum swinging and the "Focus" combat system.

Entry 02 | 2020

Miles Morales

A winter-set exploration of Harlem. Miles is forced to protect New York alone while Peter is in Symkaria. He battles Roxxon Energy and the Tinkerer (Phin Mason), discovering his bio-electric "Venom" powers and invisibility.

Visual Engine

Launch title for PS5. Showcased 60fps Ray Tracing and zero-second load times.

Core Loop

Added AOE electricity attacks and high-tech stealth camouflage.

Entry 03 | 2023

Marvel's Spider-Man 2

Dual protagonists. Peter and Miles face Kraven the Hunter and the arrival of the Symbiote. The map doubles in size, including Brooklyn and Queens. The story explores the corruption of the Black Suit and Harry Osborn's transformation into Venom.

Visual Engine

Full PS5 exclusivity. Enhanced asset streaming for 3x faster traversal.

Core Loop

Web Wings for gliding and instant character switching via SSD streaming.
