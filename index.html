Overview

The Modern Era

The "Spider-Man game saga" refers to the critically acclaimed Insomniac Games Universe (Earth-1048). While there are dozens of older games dating back to 1982, the current "Saga" is defined by its interconnected, cinematic narrative.

History

Marvel's Spider-Man

A 23-year-old Peter Parker, who has been Spider-Man for eight years, helps the police arrest Wilson Fisk. This creates a power vacuum filled by the "Inner Demons," led by Mister Negative.

Peter’s mentor, Dr. Otto Octavius, forms the Sinister Six. After the release of Devil's Breath, Peter makes the heartbreaking choice to save the city over his dying Aunt May.
